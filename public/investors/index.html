Getting a cosmetic product ready to sell is slow and easy to get wrong.

Every cosmetic product sold in the UK needs a Product Information File and a safety assessment from a qualified assessor. Small brands often do this for the first time, alone.

The rules are spread out

Label requirements, UK and EU rules and safety documents sit in different places, and they change.

Gaps show up late

A missing label item is easy to miss until the packaging is designed or printed.

Safety steps are a project of their own

Finding a safety assessor or a lab, and sending them complete information, takes time brands do not have.

One place to get product data checked and the file built.

Labelring runs every product through a rulebook of UK and EU cosmetics rules. The same rulebook is applied to every label scanned or generated.

  1. Enter product data

    The brand adds ingredients, claims, packaging and label details on the platform.

  2. See what is missing

    Labelring checks the data against the rulebook and flags missing or unclear information.

  3. Revise

    The brand fixes the flagged items and resubmits.

  4. Get the PIF file

    Labelring assembles the Product Information File from the completed data.
